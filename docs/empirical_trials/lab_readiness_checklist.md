# Laboratory Access and Trial-Planning Checklist

Because no physical laboratory or experimental trial has been arranged yet, the following practical steps must be completed before any data collection begins.

## 1. Facility and Equipment Sourcing
- [ ] **Controlled Environment:** Secure access to a temperature and humidity-controlled storage chamber or incubator capable of maintaining setpoints (e.g., 4°C).
- [ ] **Environmental Logging:** Procure calibrated continuous data loggers for Temperature and Relative Humidity.
- [ ] **Measurement Instrumentation:** Procure a calibrated high-precision laboratory balance (minimum 0.01g readability) for weight loss measurements.
- [ ] **Packaging Equipment:** Secure access to heat sealers or modified atmosphere packaging (MAP) gas flushing equipment, if applicable.

## 2. Materials Sourcing
- [ ] **Commodity Sourcing:** Establish a direct supplier for fresh strawberries to ensure known harvest time, cultivar, and immediate field-heat removal. Retail purchase is discouraged due to unknown prior thermal history.
- [ ] **Packaging Materials:** Procure exact, spec-verified polymer films (do not use generic household films for scientific trials).

## 3. Protocol and Data Registration
- [ ] **Domain Expert Sign-Off:** Execute the `expert_approval_checklist.md` to finalize the endpoint and methodology.
- [ ] **Protocol ID Assignment:** Register the finalized methodology with a unique ID in the system.
- [ ] **Data Pipeline Verification:** Ensure operators understand how to use `data_capture_template.json` to upload the raw data securely into the system.

## Recommended Next Practical Step
**Locate and partner with an academic postharvest physiology laboratory or an agricultural extension facility.** Attempting to conduct rigorous shelf-life packaging trials without baseline temperature-controlled infrastructure and precision measurement tools will result in the data failing the system's `REAL_EMPIRICAL` Quality Gates (specifically Gates C, D, E, and G).
